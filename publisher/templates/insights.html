{{ needs | upper }} feature

{% if section == "tuning" %}

Silence the noise at its source

The rule-tuning advisor finds alert patterns that {{ product_name }} closed again and again and no analyst disputed, such as the same backup script, installer or admin tool, and writes the Elastic rule exception for each one, ready to paste.

  • Fewer alerts, before triage even starts
  • Exact conditions: program, parent, account, and host when it is only one
  • Never suggested for credential access, lateral movement, impact or exfiltration rules
{% elif section == "suppressions" %}

Stop re-investigating what your team already closed

When your analysts keep marking the same pattern as benign, such as the same program started by the same parent under the same account, {{ product_name }} suggests a suppression. Create it with a reason and an expiry, and matching alerts are closed at once, without the model, with the suppression named on each one.

  • Suggested from your analysts' feedback, never from the model alone
  • Every suppression expires and counts its hits; revoke it in one click
  • Never applied to credential access, lateral movement, impact or exfiltration
{% elif section == "trends" %}

See triage improve week by week

Weekly verdicts, how often your analysts agree with {{ product_name }}, and how long each alert takes, for the last 12 weeks.

  • Spot a rule or a model change that made things worse
  • Show your team the agreement rate rising as they give feedback
{% else %}

The report your manager asks for

One page per week, month or quarter: alerts triaged, closed automatically, sent to a person, true positives found, analyst time saved, the noisiest rules, and how often analysts agreed. Print it, save it as PDF, or have it e-mailed every month.

  • Justify the budget with numbers from your own alerts
  • Per-customer reports with your logo in the MSP edition
